        What the converter does
        

          Reads v, vt, vn, f, o, g and usemtl. Triangles become degenerate quads, quads stay quads, and larger polygons are fan-triangulated, since Minecraft's entity renderer draws quads. Vertices are written interleaved, 8 numbers each (x y z u v nx ny nz), with a "q" list of quad indices. Lines, points and materials' colours are ignored; give the layer a texture or a colour in layers.json instead.
