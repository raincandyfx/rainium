Features

  • Any OBJ
  • Every material
  • Your textures
  • Real glow
  • Any player

Bring a model from any 3D program and Rainium splits it into parts by object and material. Give each part its own texture, colour, style and glow, then choose exactly who wears it.

Materials become parts

An OBJ is split by object and material, so each piece is its own layer. The whole model stays together in a folder: hide it, resize it or move it in one go.

Any texture, any colour

Give each part its own PNG, shift its colours round the wheel with Hue, and set its opacity. A texture packed in a .glb is used automatically.

Emissive glow

Set a part to glow and it ignores world light, so trims, halos and eyes shine at night. Combine glow with see-through for soft light effects.

On any player

Show each layer on just you, everyone, other players, a list of names or armor stands. It all happens on your screen, so nobody else needs the mod.

Fully customizable

Every setting has a live preview on your player, a tooltip, and a box for typing exact values. Undo is always there.

Attach point
Body, head, body without leaning, left or right arm, left or right leg.
Size, position, angle
Drag, or type exact numbers for each axis.
Style
Solid, see-through, glow, or see-through glow.
Hue and opacity
Recolour any texture without editing the image.
Movement
Sway as you run and turn, from Still to Bouncy, with fine-tuning.
Mirrored copy
Model one wing, get two. Works on arms and legs too.
Hide while gliding
Keep wings and capes out of the way when you glide.
Shown on
Per layer and per folder: you, everyone, listed players, armor stands.

Particle emitters

Layers can give off sparkles, embers, hearts and more. See particles

Share a look

Guide

Guide

From a model file to wearing it. You never need to touch a config file.

  1. 1

    Open the editor

    Press K in game, or use /rainium or Mod Menu's Configure button. You can rebind the key in Controls.

  2. 2

    Try the example halo

    Rainium comes with a Gold halo that starts switched off. Click the square next to Gold halo in the layer list.

  3. 3

    Add your own model

    Click Open rainium folder and drop in an .obj, .gltf or .glb with its textures. Pick it on the Add tab, line it up on the preview, then add it.

  4. 4

    Make it yours

    • Look: texture, style, glow, hue and opacity.
    • Placement: attach point, size, position and angle.
    • Behaviour: mirrored copy, sway, hide while gliding.
    • Shown on: which players and armor stands.
Hover for helpEvery setting's name has a short explanation.

Particles

Particles

  • Sparkles
  • Embers
  • Hearts
  • Trail
  • Aura
  • Empty emitter

A layer can give off particles instead of being a model. Start from a preset on the Add tab, then shape every part of it.

Picture

Minecraft's own particles (flame, sparkle, heart, note, smoke, petal and more) or any PNG of yours.

When

Always, only while moving, only while standing still, or more the faster you move.

Where

A point, ball, box or ring around your body, head, arms or legs, at any size.

How they move

Direction, speed, spread, gravity, drag and spin, plus size over time and fading.

Move with me

Particles travel along with you, or stay where they appeared and leave a trail.

Converters

To convert without opening the game, use the tools in tools/:

  • obj-converter.html runs in your browser, and your model never leaves your computer.

Share a look

Open a folder in the layer list and click Export as zip. It includes the models and textures it uses. Your friend drops it in their config/rainium folder and adds it from Add › Shared sets. Who you show it on stays with you.

Questions

Questions

Can other players see my cosmetics?

No. Rainium is client-side, so only you see what you set up. To share a look, send someone an exported zip.

Does it work on servers?

Yes. It only runs on your side, so it works on any server and nothing needs installing there.

What does "any player" mean?

On your screen, your cosmetics can appear on everyone, other players, a list of player names, or armor stands with a name tag you choose. Set it for everything in Settings › Visibility, or per layer under Shown on.

Will my OBJ's materials carry over?

Each material becomes its own part, so you can texture and style it separately. Put the PNG textures in the rainium folder with the model; a texture packed inside a .glb is picked up automatically.

How do updates work?

Once per launch, Rainium checks GitHub for a newer version for your Minecraft version, downloads and checks it, and installs it when you close the game. Only main releases (like 0.4.0) install automatically, unless you switch on Include dev builds in Settings › Updates.

Does Rainium collect any data?

No. Its only network request is the optional update check, which lets GitHub see your IP address. Folder paths in the editor show your in-game name instead of your computer's account name, so screenshots are safe to share.

Where are my files?

In .minecraft/config/rainium/. Open rainium folder in the editor takes you there. If a file you edited by hand has a problem, Rainium keeps your last working setup and explains what's wrong.

Can I translate it?

Yes, please. Copy en_us.json to your language's code, translate the right side of each line, and send it in an issue.
