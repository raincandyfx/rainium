Features

  • Any model
  • Every material
  • Your textures
  • Real glow
  • Anyone you see

Import an OBJ, glTF or GLB and Rainium keeps it in pieces, one part per object and material. Each part gets its own texture, colour, light and motion, and you decide who wears the result.

One part per material

Every material in your OBJ becomes its own part, grouped in a folder so the model still moves, hides and resizes as one.

Textures and colour

Give each part any PNG. Hue swings its colours round the wheel, and opacity fades it out. Textures packed in a .glb load by themselves.

Glow in the dark

Glowing parts ignore world light, so halos, trims and eyes stay bright at night. Mix glow with see-through for soft light.

On anyone you see

You, everyone, other players, a list of names, or armor stands. It's drawn on your screen only, so nobody else needs the mod.

Tune everything

Every number has a live preview on your player, a tooltip, and a box to type an exact value. Undo is one key away.

Attach point
Body, head, body without leaning, either arm or either leg.
Size, position, angle
Drag, or type exact numbers for each axis.
Style
Solid, see-through, glow, or see-through glow.
Hue and opacity
Recolour any texture without editing the image.
Movement
Sways as you run and turn, from Still to Bouncy, with fine-tuning.
Mirrored copy
Model one wing, get two. Works on arms and legs too.
Hide while gliding
Keeps wings and capes out of the way when you glide.
Shown on
Per layer or per folder: you, everyone, named players, armor stands.

Guide

Four steps from a model file to wearing it. No config files.

  1. 1

    Open the editor

    Press K in game. /rainium and Mod Menu's Configure button work too, and the key can be rebound in Controls.

  2. 2

    Try the example

    A Gold halo comes with the mod, switched off. Click the square next to Gold halo in the layer list to show it.

  3. 3

    Bring your model

    Click Open rainium folder and drop in your model and its textures. Pick it on the Add tab, line it up on the preview, and add it.

  4. 4

    Make it yours

    • Look: texture, style, hue, opacity
    • Placement: attach point, size, angle
    • Behaviour: mirroring, sway, gliding
    • Shown on: who wears it
Hover for helpEvery setting's name explains itself in a tooltip.
Type exact valuesClick any number to type it instead of dragging.
Undo anythingCtrl+Z and Ctrl+Y, or the buttons in the top bar.

Particles

A layer can be an emitter instead of a model. Start from a preset, or an empty one, then shape when, where and how it fires.

  • Sparkles
  • Embers
  • Hearts
  • Trail
  • Aura

Emitter settings

Picture
Minecraft's own particles (flame, sparkle, heart, note, smoke, petal and more) or any PNG of yours. Style, hue and opacity work like on models.
When
Always, only while moving, only while standing still, or more the faster you go. Set how many per second and how long each lasts.
Where
A point, ball, box or ring around your body, head, arms or legs, at any size.
Motion
Direction, speed, spread, gravity (negative floats up), drag and spin, plus size over time and fading.
Trail or follow
Particles travel with you, or stay where they appeared and leave a trail.

Models

OBJglTFGLB

Nearly every 3D program exports these. Blender's defaults work as they are.

Your file

An .obj, .gltf or .glb in the rainium folder, with its PNG textures beside it.

Split into parts

Rainium separates it by object and material, with a live preview while you set its size and angle.

Style each part

  • Featherstexture, solid
  • Trimglow
  • Veilsee-through, 60%

Convert outside the game

The tools/ folder has two converters:

  • obj-converter.html runs in your browser. Your model never leaves your computer.
  • mesh_convert.py handles OBJ, glTF and GLB from the command line (Python 3.8+).

Share a look

Open a folder in the layer list and click Export as zip; models and textures come along. Your friend drops it into their config/rainium folder and adds it from Add › Shared sets. Who you show it on stays private.

    Questions

    Can other players see my cosmetics?

    Rainium

    No. I'm client-side, so only you see what you set up. To share a look, send someone an exported zip.

    Does it work on servers?

    Rainium

    Yes. I only run on your side, so I work on any server and nothing needs installing there.

    What does "anyone you see" mean?

    Rainium

    On your screen, your cosmetics can appear on everyone, other players, a list of player names, or armor stands with a name tag you choose. Set it for everything in Settings › Visibility, or per layer under Shown on.

    Will my OBJ's materials carry over?

    Rainium

    Each material becomes its own part, so you can texture and style it separately. Put the PNG textures in the rainium folder with the model; a texture packed inside a .glb is picked up automatically.

    How do updates work?

    Rainium

    Once per launch I check for a newer release for your Minecraft version, download and verify it, and install it when you close the game. Your layers and settings are kept. Turn this off in Settings › Updates.

    Do you collect any data?

    Rainium

    No. My only network request is the optional update check, which lets GitHub see your IP address. Folder paths in the editor show your in-game name instead of your computer's account name, so screenshots are safe to share.

    Where are my files?

    Rainium

    In .minecraft/config/rainium/. Open rainium folder in the editor takes you there. If a file you edited by hand has a problem, I keep your last working setup and explain what's wrong.

    Can I translate you?

    Rainium

    Yes, please. Copy en_us.json to your language's code, translate the right side of each line, and send it in an issue.
